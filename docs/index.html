How the tracker works

Econ Light Monitor uses nighttime lights as a proxy for changes in local activity, infrastructure intensity, and regional momentum. It then combines that signal with official BLS labor-market data to produce more interpretable U.S. nowcasts.

The platform is best used as a directional economic indicator for finding where activity appears to be strengthening, weakening, or diverging from labor-market trends.

Best for: economic development, regional research, investment scouting, and local growth monitoring.
